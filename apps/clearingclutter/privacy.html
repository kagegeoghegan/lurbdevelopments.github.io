Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

ClearingClutter is developed and operated by Lurb Developments. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Photo Library Access

ClearingClutter requests read and write access to your photo library solely to display photos and videos for review and to delete items you explicitly choose to remove. The app does not transmit any photos, videos, or metadata to any server. All processing happens entirely on your device.

2. Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data on your device only:

  • A list of photo identifiers you have already reviewed, used to power the Unseen Only feature
  • Session and all-time statistics such as the number of photos deleted and estimated storage freed
  • Your in-app settings and preferences

None of this data is linked to your identity or transmitted off your device.

3. In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Purchase management is handled through your Apple ID settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with analytics platforms, advertising networks, or tracking services. The only third-party service involved is Apple's App Store for processing the optional in-app purchase, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions? Reach us at support@lurbdevelopments.com
