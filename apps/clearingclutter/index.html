Features

Your camera roll, finally under control.

Built for anyone with a phone full of photos they never got around to sorting. Swipe through your library at whatever pace you want, batch review what you flagged, and confirm deletions when you are ready.

Swipe to Keep or Delete

Swipe right to keep a photo, swipe left to queue it for deletion. Tap the on-screen buttons if you prefer. Either way, nothing is permanently deleted until you review and confirm the batch. Change your mind at any point before then.

Batch Review

After every set number of photos you are taken to a review screen. See everything you flagged for deletion, recover any items you want to keep, and confirm the deletion with a single tap. Batch size is adjustable from 10 to 100.

Unseen Only Mode

Turn on Unseen Only and the app will only show you photos you have not reviewed yet. Your progress is saved between sessions so you can work through your entire library gradually without ever repeating yourself.

Smart Sort Options

Sort by newest, oldest, largest, or smallest file first. Largest First surfaces your biggest videos and photos so you can free up the most space as fast as possible. Shuffle mode randomizes the order each session. Sorts combine with Unseen Only for precise control.

Album Filter

Restrict a session to a specific album such as Screenshots, WhatsApp, or any custom album. Only photos in that album will appear. Great for targeting one category at a time without touching the rest of your library.

Storage Freed Counter

A running counter shows exactly how much storage you have freed during the session. The review screen shows how much the current batch will free before you confirm. Satisfying to watch tick up.

Photos and Videos

Swipe through photos, videos, or both in the same session. Video files are clearly marked so you know what you are looking at before you decide to keep or delete.

Session and All-Time Stats

The home screen shows your all-time total of items deleted and storage freed across every session. Tap the clipboard during a session to see stats for the current run, including how many photos you reviewed, deleted, and how much space was recovered.

ClearingClutter Pro

The first 100 deletions are free. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited deletions permanently. No subscription, no recurring charges.
